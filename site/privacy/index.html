Privacy

The site counts visits. supragnosis does not.

This website uses Google Analytics. The app, the server and the CLI have no analytics of any kind.

This website

supragnosis.dev counts its visits with Google Analytics: the pages you view, the site that sent you here, whether you copy an install command and which one, and your rough location and device, kept in cookies Google sets. It helps tell what people read and how they choose to install. A copy of these pages served anywhere else counts nothing.

To be left out, block cookies for this site, or use Google's opt-out browser add-on.

supragnosis itself

The app, the server and the CLI report nothing about you, how you use them or what they hold, and send nothing to Google. They go online only to fetch the search model once, and to reach the servers and hubs you connect them to.
