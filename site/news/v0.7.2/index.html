supragnosis v0.7.2

Upgrade the app and the server, then restart once; in full screen the title bar meets the edge.

Changes since v0.7.1.

Upgrade the app and the server, then restart once; in full screen the title bar meets the edge. brew upgrade, then supragnosis restart. Only the app changed; the server is rebuilt with the version, and a restart keeps This Mac from offering the update. The store, the sync protocol, the MCP tools and status --json are unchanged.

In full screen the title bar meets the window's edge

The title bar keeps room at its leading end for the traffic lights, and drops it in full screen, where macOS hides them. The app told a page about full screen only when the window went in or out of it, so a page opened while the window was already full screen - Settings or Setup opened from it, the graph come back to, a reload - kept the room, and the bar hung away from the edge with nothing beside it. Each page now also asks the window when it loads, and that first answer lands without the padding's animation, so the bar does not slide in.
