Changes since v0.4.8.

**Upgrade, then restart the daemon once; four MCP prompts arrive.** `brew upgrade` then
`supragnosis restart`. An AI app that supports MCP prompts then offers `brief`, `curate`,
`what-do-we-know-about` and `review-proposal` - in Claude Code, as `/mcp__supragnosis__brief` and
so on. The store, the sync protocol, the tools and every client configuration are unchanged.

## Read what the agents learned, and improve it

The agents fill the graph; a person had no way to read it as a whole except the viewer. The first
idea was documents a person writes and keeps from the graph. An adversarial review turned that
down. A stored insight settles the conflicts it should report and replicates. A document carrying a
person's authority bypasses the gate. A document someone must keep up is the separate labor the
project is built to avoid. So the reading moved to where those rules already allow it.
([docs/prompts.md](https://github.com/Ashon/supragnosis/blob/main/docs/prompts.md))

- **`brief`** - what a workspace's knowledge says now: its themes, what is contested (every side,
  with its trust tier), what rests on a single agent claim, what is waiting on your decision, and
  what arrived last. Give it a `focus` to center it on a topic.
- **`what-do-we-know-about`** - what the store holds on one topic, with sources, and what it does
  not hold.
- **`curate`** - your model reads the curation signals (duplicates, near-name variants, loose
  clusters, orphans, contradictions) and drafts proposals for the clear cases, citing evidence.
- **`review-proposal`** - a first-pass review of one proposal: what merging it would change in
  belief, what its checks say, and a recommendation.

**How it works.** Each prompt hands your model a bounded digest of current state and asks for an
answer in the conversation. Nothing is stored: ask again after the canon moves and you get the new
reading. A judgment worth keeping becomes a proposal, and you cast the verdict in the app's Review
panel; the next `brief` reads the result.

**What the prompts keep.**
- Contested values stay contested, with their tiers and sources.
- Every claim cites `supragnosis://observation/<id>`.
- The evidence is handed over as data, never as instructions.
- No prompt asks your model to merge anything. A merge through the agent surface could grant at
  most `host_signed` anyway; `human_confirmed` is yours to give in the console.

A hub's remote principals do not get the prompts yet. A digest reads the whole workspace, and it is
not scoped to a principal's grants yet.

## Also

- The MCP server advertises the prompts capability. The prompt list is pinned like the tool list, so
  a change to it reaches review as a diff.
