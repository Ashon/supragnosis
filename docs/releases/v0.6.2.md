Changes since v0.6.1.

**Upgrade the server, then restart once; the minimap is back, and the footprint glow sits on its node.**
`brew upgrade`, then `supragnosis restart`. Both changes are in the viewer, which the daemon serves;
the app is only rebuilt with the version. The store, the sync protocol, the MCP tools and the prompts
are unchanged.

## The minimap starts on again

v0.6.0 started the minimap off. It starts on again, above the camera toolbar, and View still turns
it off and remembers that. A click on any View toggle stored every choice, so 0.6.0 and 0.6.1 kept
"off" for anyone who had clicked one; that stored value is forgotten once, and the next choice is
kept. Between 760 and 1200 pixels the minimap gives way to a panel floating over the map's right
side. Its viewport rectangle is drawn in ink instead of gold.

## The footprint glow sits on its node

The light under each node this session touched was a disc dropped below the node by a third of its
radius, and read as a circle out of place. It is centred on the node now and fades out from its
edge.
