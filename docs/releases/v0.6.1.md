Changes since v0.6.0.

**Upgrade, then restart once; the map fits its window, and the app finds apps installed through nvm.**
`brew upgrade` (the server and the app), then `supragnosis restart`. The store, the sync protocol,
the MCP tools and the prompts are unchanged.

## The map fits its window

v0.6.0 put the panels beside the map instead of over it, but the camera still framed the graph to
the whole canvas, as when the panels floated. A small workspace then zoomed to the cap and ran under
both panels, its outer nodes cut by the window's edge. Framing now uses the map's window between the
panels, with room on the right for the label a node carries, and the zoom buttons and the minimap
work in the same window. The viewer is the daemon's, so the restart brings it.

## The app finds AI apps installed through a Node version manager

An app opened from the Finder runs with a short `PATH`, so the Settings page called Codex and Gemini
CLI "Not installed" when they were installed through nvm - or fnm, volta, mise - though
`supragnosis connect` in a terminal found them. The app now reads your login shell's `PATH` once at
launch, in the background, and gives it to every CLI call it makes; when the shell does not answer
within five seconds, the app's own `PATH` stands as before
([docs/client-connect.md](../client-connect.md) Section 5).
