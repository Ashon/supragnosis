Changes since v0.6.2.

**Upgrade the server and the app, then restart once; one typeface everywhere, and regions are
clouds.** `brew upgrade` (the server and the app), then `supragnosis restart`. The store,
the sync protocol, the MCP tools and the prompts are unchanged. The daemon serves its fonts under
`/fonts/`, on the local console and on a hub's read tier alike, and is about 3 MB larger for them.

## One typeface, shipped with the product

The viewer and the app's Settings page loaded no font. Their content security policies allowed none,
so the IBM Plex Mono they named for identifiers was drawn as SF Mono, and reading text took each
system's own face: SF Pro with Apple SD Gothic Neo's Hangul on a Mac, Segoe UI with Malgun Gothic on
Windows. A Korean name and an English one on the same line came from two designs.

The daemon now ships Pretendard, whose Hangul and Latin are one design, and IBM Plex Mono, and
serves them itself; the policies allow the page's own fonts and no other host's. Reading text,
controls and the names of things are Pretendard; ids, relation kinds and commands are Plex Mono.
Pretendard is served as its author publishes it, in 92 files a page fetches only as its text needs
them. The map draws node and region names in Pretendard and relation labels in Plex Mono, and draws
again once a face has loaded. The Settings page carries the Latin files of both, and its last
off-scale sizes are back on the scale. Observation text, often Korean, has more room between lines.
The rules are in docs/design-system.md Section 4.

## A region is a cloud

The active node's regions were its hulls filled flat, a slab with a crisp rim, and a hub's contexts
read as a patchwork. A region is now one soft ellipse along its members, in its context's color,
densest over them and fading out with no edge. The minimap draws the same clouds.

## Also

- The Settings page's selected section has its surface again. Its stylesheet read a color it never
  declared; a new test fails on any style that reads an undeclared color.
- The install script no longer says the prebuilt binary searches by keyword only. It carries local
  semantic search, and its model is fetched once by `supragnosis service install` or
  `supragnosis embed fetch`.
- The landing at supragnosis.dev was rebuilt: seven sections, and a stage that follows one fact from
  a note to an answer in the viewer's own marks. It serves its fonts itself.
