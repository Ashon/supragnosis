Changes since v0.5.2.

**Upgrade the daemon and the app together, then restart once; the viewer is redrawn.** `brew
upgrade` (the server and the app), then `supragnosis restart`. The store, the sync protocol, the MCP
tools and the prompts are unchanged. The viewer gains one read path, `/api/search`, which a hub's
read tier answers only from 0.6.0: a newer viewer on an older hub says the search failed and the rest
of the page works. The app's Review menu opens the daemon's Review page, which an older daemon does
not have, so upgrade both.

Everything below is the viewer, the app's Settings page and the landing, built to one design
([docs/design-system.md](../design-system.md)), whose Section 1 measures what was wrong with them.

## The map shows who said it

A node supported by a person's confirmation and one supported only by an unverified source looked
alike until each was opened. Now a ring draws the node's trust: the highest effective tier among the
observations that support it, evaluated by this node, never a claimed one. Unverified is dashed,
Agent has no ring, Host signed is thin, Human confirmed is gold; the legend says what a ring reads,
and a scope with one tier draws no rings and says which. The ring meant six things before; five of
them - contested, this session's footprint, selection, a merge preview, a bridge in group mode - are
drawn another way, and only the one-second activity pulse is still a ring.

A contested node carries a mark that no zoom, label or dimming hides, and the status bar counts the
contested nodes of the scope whatever the legend hides. A type keeps its color in every workspace
instead of following the view's order. Regions are drawn for the node you point at or select, named
by two of its members; one hub no longer titles five regions after itself. The local console
remembers each workspace's layout, so a known workspace appears at once instead of behind a loader.

## Everything that waits for a decision is on one page

Proposals and the review queue were two tabs of a dock whose labels were cut off. The Review page
(View > Review, Cmd+2; the tray's "Review (N)..." while something waits) lists what needs you, what
was decided, suggestions and health. It shows one item at a time, with its belief diff and its checks
on screen next to the decision, and decides that item alone; J and K move, A and R accept and reject.
A merge a check blocks is listed with its reason and offers only Reject, and a recall asks twice. The
node inspector is a sheet on the right, where it no longer covers the part of the map it describes.

The Types tab's rows moved into the filters, and the Peers tab's into Settings > Sync, with a sync
pill on the status bar.

## Search answers like the agents' search

The search field filtered node names by substring. Cmd+K or "/" now asks `/api/search`, which calls
the function behind `search_knowledge` and lists what it answers in its order - entities, then
observations - under the label of the surface that answered (`hybrid` or `keyword`). Enter focuses
an entity or opens an observation's card, and while the list is open the map lights exactly the
entities it returned. An entity merged away is answered by its canonical id, and an empty answer says
absence is unknown. On a hub's read tier the search covers the reader's grants and nothing else.

## Surfaces, not lines

The viewer drew 53 borders and spent the human-confirmed gold on headings, hover and frames. Now the
header, the panels and the status bar are one frame and the map is a window in it; areas are told
apart by tone, and a line is drawn only where it carries a fact. Gold means selected, human
confirmed, waiting for you, or keyboard focus.

- The panel toggles are at the title bar's right (`[` and `]` as before).
- With the right side empty, a one-line strip shows the scope's newest observation; clicking it opens
  the Log.
- The camera is one toolbar. The minimap starts off; View turns it on and remembers it.
- The boundaries' control is in View, still cycled by b. Region names are 13px.
- Under 1200 pixels one panel shows at a time; under 760 a panel is a sheet from the bottom.

The app's Settings page and the landing take the same surfaces.

## Also

- Every console write - accept, reject, confirm, propose, name a group - reports what it did, or the
  server's reason for refusing, where it was made.
- Every control is a button with a visible focus ring, tab lists move with the arrow keys, nothing is
  drawn below 11px, faint text meets 4.5:1, and under reduced motion nothing moves.
- Esc closes the inspector. Settings > About names the source again, and the splash points at
  Settings instead of tray items that had moved.
