Changes since v0.6.3.

**Upgrade the server, then restart once; every region drawn, workspaces together, links
untangled.** `brew upgrade`, then `supragnosis restart`. Every change is in the viewer,
which the daemon serves; the app is only rebuilt with the version. The store, the sync protocol, the
MCP tools and the prompts are unchanged.

## Every region is drawn, mixed in OKLCH

At rest every region is now a faint cloud, and while a node is active its own regions come up and the
rest fade; only the active node's regions are named, so node labels keep their places. A stored
"all regions: off" is forgotten once. Every region shares one lightness and chroma in OKLCH, its hue
from its context, and where clouds overlap they are mixed in OKLCH instead of by the canvas, whose
sRGB compositing met in greys and browns. A cloud wider than about half the window fades, and
overlapping clouds no longer stack toward opaque: zoomed into a cluster, the clouds had covered almost
the whole window, and now leave the names readable.

## Workspaces hold together

With every workspace in scope and the boundaries on "workspace", a workspace's loose pieces had
drifted across the map and its outline enclosed mostly empty space. Nodes beyond a workspace's
natural radius are now pulled toward its centre, workspaces whose spreads overlap are pushed apart as
wholes, and nodes of different workspaces repel each other less. On the author's store the farthest
node of a small workspace came from 2292 to 530 units out, and no node lies nearer another
workspace's centre than its own. Layouts remembered for a scope of several workspaces are forgotten
once.

## Links fit their density

Every link had one length and one strength, and on a large graph linked nodes sat about twice their
rest length apart, so long links crossed each cluster. A link's length now grows with the smaller
degree of its ends and with how crowded they are, its pull falls with that degree, and the end with
fewer links moves more: leaves gather around their hubs and hubs keep room between them. On the
author's store, crossings per link fell from 6.5 to 2.8.

## Also

- Node labels are thinned by the nodes in view, not the graph's count, so zooming into a large graph
  names its cluster progressively instead of only past 1.4x.
