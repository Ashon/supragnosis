Changes since v0.6.5.

**Upgrade the server and the app, then restart once; Setup in the app, and a login service on Linux.**
`brew upgrade`, then `supragnosis restart`, and open the app. The store and the sync protocol are
unchanged. One MCP tool description changed (`propose` may name a merge's entities by name).
`status --json` gains fields and keeps its schema number.

## Setup, in the app

v0.6.5 gave the first run a command, `supragnosis setup`. Its page in the app was left for this
release, so a person who installed the app still met a graph with nothing in it and no next step.
The app now opens on **Setup** at the first launch, and again while the daemon or the apps step
waits: the four steps of `setup --events`, drawn and nothing else. The plan is shown first. Each
item is a checkbox: Start at Login, each AI app to connect, the model. One "Set up" applies the
plan, and Return presses it. An item that replaces what the person set up starts unchecked: an
app's "Switch to the bridge", or the daemon's "Take over". While the plan runs, each act's lines
stream into its step, and the foot counts the steps. The last step suggests a question about the
person's real work and waits for the first entry it makes. Restart Claude Desktop is one click. The
tray offers "Finish Setup..." while something waits, and Settings > About always has "Open Setup".

Settings says each fact once:

- Version drift is one card with the only Restart.
- A section's dot appears only when the section needs attention.
- The server list is a list.
- Claude Code's row has a switch, "Save the why behind each commit". It turns the capture hook on
  and off; `connect claude-code --no-capture` now removes the hook and keeps the bridge.

## Linux keeps the daemon running

On Linux, `supragnosis service install` now does what it does on macOS: it generates the always-on
manager, starts it, and keeps it at every login. Before, it declined, and setup named a unit to copy
by hand. The manager is the systemd user unit `supragnosis.service`, built from the directives of
the unit deploy/systemd shipped:

- It asks `loginctl enable-linger`, so the daemon outlives a logout. Where polkit refuses, it prints
  the line an administrator has to run.
- `status`, `restart`, `stop` and `setup` recognize the unit and Homebrew's.
- A unit someone copied by hand is theirs: it is never overwritten, and `--take-over` moves it aside
  and carries its `Environment=` lines.
- Where `systemctl --user` does not work, as in a container, install says why and names
  `supragnosis serve`.

On a fresh Ubuntu 24.04 user, `setup --yes` went from an empty home to "Every step is done", and the
daemon came back after a restart with nobody logged in.

`connect` also stopped counting a Mac's app bundles on Linux. A VM can see a Mac's `/Applications`,
and setup there connected Claude Desktop and ran the Mac's VS Code CLI.

## Every screen, read again

Two passes went over every screen of the viewer, the app's pages and the site, against the
principles and then against Apple's guidelines and the measured contrast of each text
(docs/design-system.md Sections 14 and 24).

**What changed in how things read:**

- Text comes in three tiers, each at least APCA Lc 60 on every surface (body text Lc 75). Signals
  drawn as text have their own inks.
- Sizes are a Mac's 13, 12 and 11 points, in rem, so text grows: View > Make Text Bigger (Cmd+=)
  in the app, and the browser's zoom on the site.
- Before, 374 texts in the viewer read under Lc 60; now 14 do.
- Increase Contrast and forced colors are followed.

**What changed in how things look:**

- The tools follow the system's light and dark appearance. The site stays dark.
- Lines are thin and solid, with no dashes. Trust's rings are told by weight.
- Corners come from six tokens. Large acts are capsules.
- Every target is at least 28 pixels, and 44 under a finger.
- The brand gold is one gold in both appearances; on light, gold text is drawn in ink.

**What changed in how things work:**

- The title bar is a Mac toolbar. The leading end holds the filters toggle (or "< Graph" on every
  other page) and the title of the place on screen; the trailing end holds the search and the
  right panel's toggle. Review, Settings and Setup share one bar.
- The map has a List: the entities as a table that keeps the map's selection.
- Search groups its hits by how they were reached (words, meaning, a neighbor) and offers the wider
  scope.
- A contested value is settled with one act per value, asked once, and can be undone.
- Review is always in the status bar, and Needs you lists only the beliefs that tie.
- Every Log row says who wrote it and when, marked with the map's ring for its tier.
- Follow frames what changed without moving the reader. The first view opens where the knowledge
  is.
- A tooltip names a control and its key; what a control does is said on the screen.
- View holds two choices, Regions and Boundaries, with real switches, and a switch that turns off
  the single-key shortcuts.

The site's landing says the next step after install and reads on a phone. Its labels are in the
reading face, and its pictures were retaken. The principles page says what its words mean.

## The daemon says which port it got, and stops as a normal exit

A daemon asked for `--http 127.0.0.1:0` writes the address it bound to `~/.supragnosis/listening`.
SIGTERM, which `stop`, launchd, systemd and the app all send, is now a normal exit: the daemon
gives requests in flight up to three seconds and exits 0. It used to die by the signal. Both serve
the end-to-end scenarios (docs/e2e.md): A1-A4 and A8 run in CI, and the README counts them.

## Also

- `propose` turns an `entity_merge` target given by name into the id `observe` gave it, so naming
  PostgreSQL and Postgres opens a merge the integrity check does not block. A confirmation made
  from All lands in the observations' workspace.
- `status --json` names a systemd manager as type `systemd`. Its `service` object gains `path` and
  `linger`; `plist` keeps its name and holds the unit's path on Linux.
